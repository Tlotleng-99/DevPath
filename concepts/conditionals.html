🧍 Taxi or Walk?

Conditionals let your program make decisions. Different situations, different actions — just like choosing how to get home.

Try it yourself live

You have R25 in your pocket. Do you take the taxi, the bus, or walk? Make a choice and see the conditional logic.

Make a choice above to see the conditional logic...
# if/else logic will appear here

The proper cs name

A conditional (if/else/elif) lets your program choose between different paths based on a condition.

  • if money >= 25: — if true, take the taxi.
  • elif money >= 12: — else if, take the bus.
  • else: — otherwise, walk.
money = 25

if money >= 25:
    print("🚐 Taxi!")
elif money >= 12:
    print("🚌 Bus")
else:
    print("🚶 Walk")

Where they live: every decision in software — login checks, game logic, form validation — uses conditionals.

The story, slowly 🧠

1

Check the condition

Do you have enough money? That's the condition — a yes/no question the program asks.

2

If true, do this

Money >= R25? Yes! Take the taxi. The if-block runs when the condition is true.

3

Else, do that

Not enough for a taxi? Check the next option — maybe the bus works. That's the elif.

4

Otherwise, fallback

No money at all? The else-block catches everything else. You walk, eish.
